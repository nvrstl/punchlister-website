Why Punchlister

Domain knowledge first. Software second.

Most construction software is built by people who never saw a job site up close. We did. That difference shows in every detail of the product — from the structure of a site report to the way extra work gets priced.

We're a small, founder-led team in Ghent. We work directly with construction companies in a pilot program, and we build fast based on what's actually happening on site. No layers between you and the user.

How we work

Three things we hire for.

02

The process

Short, honest, and with real people.

2

A conversation with one of the founders. About you, about Punchlister, and about the problem we're solving.

3

A practical assignment or case, as close as possible to the real work. No trick questions.

Fast and clear. We won't leave you waiting for weeks, and we always give feedback.
